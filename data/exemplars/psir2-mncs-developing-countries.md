# Topic: Discuss the role of MNCs in shaping the politics across the developing countries.

MNCs are *transnational actors* whose functioning transcends national boundaries. Their rise is described as 'supraterritoriality' and a *post-sovereign world*.

After globalisation, the world became a global village and MNCs were encouraged by *liberals* as a source of modernity — funds and technology — in developing nations.

MNCs soon started playing a role in the politics of developing nations. On one hand, they help governments carry out their functions more effectively — e.g. PPP projects and CSR funds in India.

But at the same time, MNCs are threatening the sovereignty of nations as they emerge too powerful. Political funding — politician–bureaucracy–MNC networks are seen in India, and tussles between government and MNCs are rising — e.g. Vedanta vs Government of India. MNCs like Facebook–Cambridge Analytica influenced US elections, so the influence is not limited to the developing world.

MNCs need to be regulated for sustainable development and the state's sovereignty.
