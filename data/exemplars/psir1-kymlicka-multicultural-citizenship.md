# Topic: Analyse Kymlicka's theory of multicultural citizenship.

Multiculturalism is a theory which focuses on minority rights in a liberal society. It addresses the question — how to accommodate diversity in liberal nations?

Will Kymlicka gave his theory of multicultural citizenship and voiced for granting rights to *national minorities*:
(i) self-government rights, and
(ii) cultural rights.

These rights are to be given only to national minorities, as they suffered historical injustices — e.g. tribals in India under the 6th Schedule. Other migrants are not to be given these rights, as they came by choice.

This idea is criticised by Bhikhu Parekh, who goes for "thick multiculturalism." He says there is no harm in granting rights to other minorities as well, since they are also related to their culture; he applies the 'harm principle' in granting rights. Multiculturalism is also criticised for being narrow and divisive, and against unity. But this is refuted in other theories as well — communitarianism, etc. — and Kymlicka's framework remains a foundational liberal defence of group-differentiated rights.
