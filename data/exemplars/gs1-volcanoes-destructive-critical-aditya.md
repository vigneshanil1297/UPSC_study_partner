# Topic: Despite being destructive in nature, volcanoes are critical for the existence of human life on earth. Elucidate.

Volcanoes are formed due to <u>magma plumes</u> that try to erupt through <u>fissures</u> in the Earth's crust.

[Labelled volcano cross-section diagram — vent, magma ascent, and plume chamber marked.]

**Volcanoes are very destructive**

1. Lava flows <u>kill people</u> and <u>damage property</u>. E.g. more than 10,000 killed by the Mt. Vesuvius explosion.
2. <u>GHG emissions</u> ⟹ destroy environment and increase global warming.
3. <u>Extinctions</u> as seen during the K–T boundary (65 million years ago).
4. <u>Forest fires</u> ⟹ loss of livelihoods for tribals.
5. <u>Heat waves</u> ⊕ can even trigger earthquakes — e.g. St. Helens (1981).

**However, they are a necessary force, due to importance for human life**

1. Bring <u>minerals</u> to the earth's surface — e.g. iron ore in Kudremukh.
2. <u>Black soil</u> due to weathering of igneous rock ⟹ cotton cultivation (Maharashtra).
3. <u>Resources</u> like stones of granite ⟹ used for construction.
4. <u>Geothermal</u> power capacity ⟹ uphold Panchamrit goals.
5. <u>Fertilize</u> the soil ⟹ essential for better forest renewal.
6. Occasional <u>forest fires</u> ⟹ clean foliage ⟹ stronger forest ecosystem.

Thereby the need here is to ensure that we minimize damage by volcanoes via the <u>Sendai framework</u> while reaping benefits.
