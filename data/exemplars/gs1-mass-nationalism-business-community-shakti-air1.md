The idea of <u>mass nationalism</u> was incorporated in the <u>Indian freedom struggle</u> with the advent of <u>Mahatma Gandhi</u> and his concept of <u>Satyagraha</u> based on <u>truth</u> and <u>non-violence</u>.

1. This is considered to be a <u>watershed movement</u> in the freedom struggle.
2. It shifted the focus from <u>elites</u> to the <u>masses</u>.
3. It made the freedom struggle a <u>national duty</u> for all.
4. It encouraged every section of Indian society to be a part of <u>India's growth story</u>.
5. This led to participation of:— workers, peasants, women, students, etc., which made the movement more strong, more <u>effective</u> and <u>inclusive</u>.

**Indian Business community**

During colonial rule, the British used India as a < source of raw material / market for finished goods >. This had led to the emergence of:

[2-column compare diagram]
| **Indigenous capitalist class** | **Foreign capitalist class** |
| e.g. Tatas, Birlas, Singhanias, etc. | e.g. owners of British factories |

The Indian business community did not outrightly participate in the struggle against the British, but they did realise how the British were exploiting our resources for their benefit. This led to further <u>resentment among the business class</u> also, and this ignited a process of a <u>self-sustained economy</u>.
