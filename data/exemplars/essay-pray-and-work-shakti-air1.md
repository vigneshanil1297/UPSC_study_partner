# Topic: Pray as though everything depended on God and work as though everything depended on you

Few years back, in 2019, when Chandrayaan-2 was launched, the entire internet flooded with the picture of our Hon'ble Prime Minister consoling the ISRO chief K. Sivan, as he could not control his tears, knowing that his years of hard work did not turn into a visible reality. Today, four years apart, in 2023, India has successfully placed Chandrayaan-3 in the desired orbit, on path to making India one of the top four countries of the world to land its spacecraft on the surface of the moon.

But how did this happen? Why did we fail in 2019 and what made it possible in 2023? The reasons are not definite, neither are they obvious. As it is said — "There are known knowns; there are unknown knowns; and then there are unknown unknowns." Not everything we put efforts into becomes a reality, and not everything imagined remains a dream. The actuality of an event is backed by both controllables and uncontrollables. That is why it is said that hardwork alone doesn't work; there has to be a strong emotive force driving it. In this essay we will look at how any reality is shaped by both hardwork and prayers, and how they impact the lives of millions.

"Pray as though everything depended on God…" — what does prayer mean, and what does God signify? Is there actually a metaphysical understanding to this physical world? Yes — prayers signify our thought process, our imaginative strength, our vision to conceptualise an idea. It is about imagining the unimagined. While God represents an unshakeable strength, belief system, and an emotive force that indicates nothing is impossible.

Now, the second facet: "…and work as though everything depended on you." We realise that no matter how strong the will power is, or how vivid one's vision can be, until efforts are put into it, it remains a mere discourse — a thought which is lifeless. Thus prayers along with hardwork create realities that were once unimaginable. It is said, "Every innovation is stemmed into imagination," because imagination creates a skeleton, and innovation along with hardwork converts it into a reality, i.e. a living entity.

We have lived in a patriarchal society from ancient times, where customs like the purdah system, sati and child marriage have been a norm. But when men of dignity have imagined a humane society, they have worked towards its eradication. Such was the dedication of Raja Ram Mohan Roy, who did not just imagine it, but believed in his concept as it was rooted in humanism, and combined it with his hard work and efforts to lead to the enactment of the Regulation Act banning sati, enacted by the British in 1828. Similarly, the Rakhmabai case in the history of women still remains a story of unparalleled faith coupled with relentless efforts, strength and zeal to stand for one's rights — in an era where women were considered property of man, a woman spoke out loud about her conjugal rights. It is a tale of valour, because she knew she had to fight her own battle, with the faith that no matter how difficult the path, the truth / the reality / God shall prevail.

We as humans create vision, and these visions create realities when sufficiently combined with the right input and in the right direction. Milkha Singh never got formal training, neither did his situation permit him, but did this stop him from being India's track-and-field athlete? The one who was orphaned during the partition had faith in his strength and belief in his hard work. This signifies that both "prayers" and "hardwork" go together and not in isolation. Another lesson can be drawn from the Indian cricket team's former captain Mahendra Singh Dhoni, who had not just explored his strengths and weaknesses but worked on them immensely to become one of the most successful and resilient captains the Indian team ever had.

Thus, these stories are not just stories of grit, determination and hardwork, but are equally fuelled with faith, belief and emotive perception. If we recall India's Chandrayaan case, can we attribute the failure of Chandrayaan-2 to a lack of hardwork of our scientists, or to a lack of determination that was improved during the launch of Chandrayaan-3 — or do we analyse and look at it from a different perspective? We need to look at the bigger picture: not everything is under human control. There is enough evidence to leave things on faith, and thus faith and prayers are equally important parts of failures and successes. The launch of Chandrayaan-2 was perfectly and scientifically planned, but then there are unknowns that are beyond our control, and thus the failure of Chandrayaan-2 was definitely not an academic setback.

In human life, several components have equal impact on the decisions people make, on the realities they construct within their head, and on the efforts they put into them. To pray is to keep faith and to imagine boldly; to work is to convert that vision into reality. A life that holds both — the humility to trust what lies beyond control and the discipline to labour at what lies within it — is the one that turns the unimaginable into the inevitable.
