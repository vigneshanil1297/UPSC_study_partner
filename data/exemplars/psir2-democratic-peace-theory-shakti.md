# Topic: Critically evaluate the democratic peace theory.

The **democratic peace theory** has been given by the *liberal scholars*, stating that countries which adopt democracy are more peaceful in comparison to those which don't. This has been supplemented by the **political development approach** of *Lucian Pye*.

According to the *Marxist scholars*, however, these are just **metanarratives** to establish the hegemony of Western liberal ideology based on multilateralism and democracy. The theory thus carries a normative, Eurocentric load rather than a neutral empirical law.

The best example in this regard is **India and Pakistan** — both renowned democracies of South Asia, yet they have seen two full-fledged wars (*1965, 1971*) — thereby confirming that "democracy ensures peace" is not completely correct. Though democracy ensures that government works in accordance with the wishes of the people, and obviously people don't prefer war, the so-called "peace" rather depends on the **rationality of leaders** and the sense of humanity among people, than on the regime type alone.
