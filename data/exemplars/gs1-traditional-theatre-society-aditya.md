# Topic: The traditional theatre forms of India reflect the ideals and emotions of the society, and an individual's role in the community. Discuss with examples.

Recently <u>Ramlila</u> and <u>Mudiyettu</u> were recognised as intangible heritage and are already there in the UNESCO intangible-heritage list.

**They reflect the ideals & emotions of society**

1. <u>Ramlila</u> of UP shows the importance of <u>epics</u> in the lives of people → Ramayana.
2. Theatre of <u>Krishnaleela</u> in Mathura shows the importance of <u>love</u> as a value.
3. <u>Rammam</u> of Uttarakhand shows the local emotions of <u>nature-centric life</u>.
4. Kerala theatre forms like <u>Mudiyettu</u> show the importance of the emotions of → rajsik → tamsik → satvik ways of <u>life</u> and <u>preferences</u>.

**Reflecting the individual's role in the community**

1. Role of the individual shown as <u>altruistic</u> by these values.
2. Respect for the elders promoted, as Ram for Dashratha.
3. The <u>Odisha</u> style of theatres promotes the values of marine-life centricity of human.
4. <u>Ramakoothu</u> of Tamil Nadu promotes the "<u>purushottam</u>" way of life for the individual.

Thereby theatres traditionally were a means of <u>socialization</u>. There is a need to preserve them for future generations to see and learn.
