# Topic: Evaluate the role of subsidiary alliance policy in turning 'the British Empire in India' into 'the British Empire of India'.

Advent of the British in India as a trading company, and their withdrawal as a colonial power, was possible because of their imperialist policies — one of the most important being the <u>Subsidiary Alliance</u>.

**Subsidiary Alliance**

1. This policy was put forward by <u>Richard Wellesley</u>.
2. The states which signed the Subsidiary Alliance treaty could not have an army of their own.
3. They had to station the British army in their territory, which was to be maintained by the states themselves.
4. The state could not have an independent foreign policy.
5. They could not engage with the Portuguese or French without permission of the British.
6. One British official was to be stationed in their court.

Hyderabad, Bharatpur, Satara, Jaunpur and more states fell prey to this British policy of <u>paramountcy</u>.

↓

This was further extended by the "<u>Doctrine of Lapse</u>" under Dalhousie, under which states that had signed Subsidiary Alliance were annexed on grounds of no biological heir / maladministration.

Thus the Subsidiary Alliance led to annexation of various territories and further led to expansion of British control, and helped in establishing the British Empire.
