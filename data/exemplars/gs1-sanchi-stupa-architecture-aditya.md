Sanchi Stupa is an important stupa located in Madhya Pradesh and is based on the ideals of Buddhism.

[Labelled cross-section diagram of the SANCHI STUPA — Chhatri (top), Harmika, Anda (dome), Medhi (drum/base), and the Pradakshinapath (circumambulatory path) marked around it.]

**Historical significance**

1. Built during the <u>Gupta period</u> ⟹ shows the artistic brilliance of that age.
2. Use of <u>stone vedika</u> shows the presence of kings' patronage, due to the transport of stone.
3. Buddhism getting patronage from kings is shown.

**Architectural significance**

1. Shows the <u>personification of Buddha</u> → legs as Medhi, body as Anda.
2. <u>Chhatri</u> signified the Three Jewels → Buddha, Dhamma, Sangha.
3. Upper and lower <u>pradakshinapath</u> for Mahayana and Hinayana.
4. <u>Jataka</u> stories on the vedika ⟹ carving brilliance.

**Inspiration for future architecture**

1. The gardens of the stupa are said by <u>A.L. Basham</u> to have inspired the Chahar Bagh.
2. The <u>torana</u> inspired the gates of arch and trabeate styles as well.
3. <u>Domes</u> of the future were inspired from the design of the <u>Anda</u>, as said by <u>Romila Thapar</u>.
4. Overall, the <u>pradakshinapath</u> saw the creation of future parikramas and corridors.

Thereby Sanchi Stupa has a very strong impact on modern <u>architecture</u> and <u>culture</u>.
